Quick Answers

World Cup coverage — answered.

What areas do you serve for World Cup events?

We serve all of Metro Atlanta — DeKalb, Gwinnett, Fulton, Clayton, and Rockdale counties. Travel is included within the metro area. For events outside this range, a travel fee may apply.

Can I add the 360° booth to my photography or video package?

Absolutely. We can bundle the 360° booth with photography, video, or both. Call or text us at 833-847-6609 and we'll put together a custom quote for your event.

How far in advance should I book?

World Cup dates in Atlanta will be in extremely high demand. We recommend booking as early as possible — ideally 4–6 weeks before your event — to guarantee your preferred package and date.

What's included in the price?

All photography packages include editing, your private online gallery, and full print release. Video packages include licensed music and color grading. 360° booth packages include setup, an attendant, props, and digital sharing. No surprise fees.

How does the deposit and payment work?

A 50% deposit is required to hold your date. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your event. We accept all major cards through our secure online booking system.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact us at least 48 hours before your event and we'll move your date at no charge, subject to availability. Your deposit stays safe and moves with you.
