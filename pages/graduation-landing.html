Quick Answers

Graduation photography — answered.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of metro Atlanta — including DeKalb, Gwinnett, Fulton, Clayton, and Rockdale counties. Travel is included within the metro area at no extra charge.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as possible. May and June dates fill quickly every year. We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks before your graduation date to guarantee your spot.

What exactly is included in the price?

All packages include a pre-session consultation, professional retouching, best-select images, a private online gallery, and a full print release. No surprise fees — what you see is what you pay.

Can I bring family to the session?

Yes. Family portraits are fully included in the Premier Experience. For Essential and Signature sessions, a few family members are welcome — just mention it when you book so we can plan accordingly.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. Just contact us at least 48 hours before your session and we'll move your date at no charge, subject to availability. Your deposit stays safe and moves with you.

How does the deposit and payment work?

A 50% deposit is required to hold your date. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your session. We accept all major cards through our secure online booking system.

Can we shoot on campus or at a specific venue?

Absolutely. We can shoot on campus, at home, at a park, or any location you choose. We'll plan the details together during your pre-shoot consultation.
